# Ochin (অচিন) design system v1

The law for every work marked `brand: ochin`. Data lives in `brand.json`; the visual reference is
`design-system.html`; `references/` holds the spreads of Ochin 01, the standard pages are judged by.

## The idea
From Lalon Fakir's song of the unknown bird that flies in and out of the cage. The bird is every
open question; the cage is what we already know. We never pretend to catch the bird: each story
shows where it flies, who chased it before, and what is still open. Wonder, not trivia. Every age.

## Who decides what
1. **Brand** (fixed): colours, faces and ladder, shapes and their meanings, finish, bird, bilingual rule, voice.
2. **Series**: masthead, trim, margins, grid, folio. Chooses from the brand; adds nothing.
3. **Section**: two of the six section colours, one illustration register, which signature leads.
4. **Spread**: the device, the composition, the crop. Fresh every time: no device twice running.
Variety lives at the bottom. A section looks different by choosing, never by inventing.

## Colour (nothing outside these ten is ever drawn)
- Core: shankha #F6EEDC ground · kajol #1E1A1D ink · sindur #D23A1E signal (a mark, never a field) · paper #EDE2CA second paper.
- Section: holud #EBA417 light · neel #27357A night, history · kolapata #2F7D46 life · padma #12807E water, machines · shapla #E07A9A people, art · mati #A9502B earth, craft.
- Inside pages: paper about half, ink a fifth, two section colours a sixth, sindur a sliver. Openers may fill with one section colour. Quiet pages: paper, ink, one sindur mark.
- Text on ground: body only on shankha (kajol, neel, mati), paper (kajol, neel), kajol (shankha, holud, shapla), neel (shankha, holud), holud (kajol, neel), shapla (kajol), mati (shankha). Heads may also use sindur, kolapata, padma on light grounds and shankha on kolapata, padma, sindur.

## Type
Galada (mark, big numbers, years, word box) · Tiro Bangla (heads, pulls; italic for the other language) ·
Anek Bangla (body, deck 300, captions, labels 600 caps tracked) · Atma (youngest readers only).
Ladder in pt: N1 96 · D1 66/70 · D2 42/46 · D3 28/32 · pull 21/28 · D4 16/21 · deck 14/20.5 · body 11/16.6 · chip 10/14.6 · cap 9.2/13.4 · label 8.4/11. A page never invents a size.
Every headline hangs from a matra rule.

## Bilingual rule
English carries the reading; Bengali carries the heart (about 85/15 by words). Always Bengali: the mark,
folio digits, each section's name beside the English eyebrow. Once per story: one Bengali line with its
English in italic beneath. One word box (শব্দ) per feature. Never paragraphs side by side.

## Shapes and what they mean (a shape used without its meaning is decoration)
- **Lal paar** (লাল পাড়), the Puja sari border: a threshold. Cover foot, section openers, back cover only.
- **Matra** (মাত্রা), the headstroke letters hang from: above every headline.
- **Kantha** (কাঁথা), old cloth stitched into new: only where something connects (a path, a timeline, a river, a word to what it names).
- **Alpana** (আলপনা), the welcome at the door: opens sections, ends stories, frames a magnified detail. One per spread.
- **The bird** (অচিন পাখি), the question: cover, the end of every story (small, on its line), back cover. One per spread. Never inside a cage.
- **Panel** (পট), cloth or a pat scroll: plain by default; a wave edge for water, scallop for petals, zigzag for mountains or stitch, steps for ghats and buildings. May fade an edge or grade into a second brand colour.
- **Festival ornaments** (alpona, kolam, rosette, band): only for ritual, craft and pattern topics; else one per issue.

## Finish
Every drawn shape: wobble, watercolour, wear, grain, paper, seeded. Large panels: grain only. Type stays sharp.

## The page
A4 210 x 297, bleed 3, margins 16, six columns, gutter 4. Spreads, not pages. Folio at the foot, outer
corner: Bengali digits, then "Ochin · issue title". Text never crosses the fold. Capacity is measured.

## Devices (one per spread, fresh)
Across the fold · magnifier (alpana ring on a detail) · exploded stack · film strip · number stack in
Galada · kantha timeline · album mount with photo corners · opposing pages · measured chart (real data only).

## Pictures
Photographs: real, credited, licence recorded, never recoloured. Illustration: opaque gouache, flat colour,
visible brush, paper showing through, brand colours. Exact things are vectors to real data. No invented portraits.

## Voice
An elder sibling at the window: curious, warm, exact. Asks before it tells, names real people, admits what
nobody knows yet.

## Brand check (code fails a page on any)
Colour outside the ten · face outside the four or size outside the ladder · text under the contrast table ·
signature over its ration · more than two section colours in a section · missing folio · overlapping text ·
a photograph without source and licence.
